Safety & Ethics
Your wellbeing comes before any feature. Here's exactly how MediBloom handles it.
Not a substitute
MediBloom helps you remember your medicines and flags risky combinations. It does not diagnose or prescribe — always check with your doctor or pharmacist first.
Your data stays here
Everything lives on this device. Nothing is sent out unless you turn on Email or Telegram reminders — and even then, only using your own account.
In an emergency
If you or someone else is having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or the nearest hospital right away — don't wait on an app.
How insights are made
"Today's Insight" and the chat assistant use clear rules on your own history — not a mystery AI, and nothing leaves your device to generate them.
Checked automatically
Every time you add a medicine, we check it against everything else you take right away — you never have to remember to ask.
Built for everyone
Simple language, a larger-text option, and it works fully offline — easy to use on any device, anywhere.
