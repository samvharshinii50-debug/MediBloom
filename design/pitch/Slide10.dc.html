MediBloom
Appendix · Data & Method
Why we chose rules over a trained model
For a safety-critical checker, explainable beats clever.
Where our data comes from
FDA openFDA — public drug label & adverse-event data
NIH RxNorm — free generic/brand name normalization
Published clinical pharmacology references — well-established interactions only
All public, all free — hand-curated for accuracy, never scraped or guessed.
Why not a trained model for safety checks
Explainable — every warning traces to a documented rule
Reliable offline — no weights, no inference cost, no hallucination risk
Zero training data needed — no patient data collected, ever
If we add personalization later
Aggregated, anonymized on-device adherence patterns (never raw medical identity) → a small, interpretable model (decision rules or logistic regression — not a deep black box) → still runs entirely on-device, and still falls back to the rule engine whenever confidence is low.
