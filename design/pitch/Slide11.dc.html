MediBloom
Appendix · Accessibility
Designed with elderly users in mind
Every challenge below maps to a specific, already-designed feature.
Small text is hard to read
Larger-text & high-contrast mode
Tiny buttons are hard to tap
Every button ≥44px, one-tap Mark Taken / Snooze / Skip
Not sure what a notification means
Optional voice read-aloud — says the medicine name & dose out loud
Typing medicine names is tedious
Autocomplete on generic & brand names, minimal typing
Living alone — a missed dose goes unnoticed
Optional Caregiver Alert — a family member is notified if a dose is missed
Unfamiliar with apps & medical jargon
Plain language everywhere, single-screen flows, no accounts or passwords
