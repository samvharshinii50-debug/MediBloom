Severe · 1
Warfarin

SEVERE
Both of these raise your bleeding risk. Taking them together makes stomach bleeding much more likely, especially if you use Ibuprofen often.

Moderate · 1
Sertraline
Ibuprofen
MODERATE
Sertraline and Ibuprofen together can also raise your bleeding risk, especially in your stomach.
What to do
Watch for unusual bruising or an upset stomach. Ask your doctor about a longer-term pain relief plan.
Mild · 1
Atorvastatin
Metformin
MILD
Safe to take together for most people. In rare cases, Atorvastatin can slightly affect blood sugar readings.
What to do
Nothing needed right now — just mention both to your doctor if you notice unusual glucose readings.
